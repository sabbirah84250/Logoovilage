1. Who we are

Logovilage is a freelance design studio based in Lisbon, Portugal. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it's used.

2. Information we collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect the name, email address, business name and message you provide. We don't collect payment details through this website.

3. How we use it

Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your enquiry and, if a project goes ahead, to communicate about that project. We don't sell or share this information with third parties for marketing.

4. Cookies

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Any strictly necessary cookies set by the hosting provider (Netlify) are used only to operate the site.

5. Data storage

Contact form submissions are stored by our hosting provider, Netlify, in line with their own privacy and security practices.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you. Contact us using the details on the Contact page to make a request.
